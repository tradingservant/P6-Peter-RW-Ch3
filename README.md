# P6-Peter-RW-Ch3

Peter · Reading & Writing Chapter 3. A colourful, tap-friendly practice game for Primary 6 English.

Theme: **natural disasters** and **We can make a difference!**

English for the exercises. Traditional Chinese for buttons, help, and short feedback.

## Play online

https://tradingservant.github.io/P6-Peter-RW-Ch3/

That address works after GitHub Pages is turned on for this repository: branch `main`, folder `/` (root). This project does not turn Pages on by itself.

## Open locally

From this folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080

You can also open `index.html` directly in a browser. The game does not need a server or a network connection. Progress (stars) stays in the browser.

On an iPhone, use Safari. Buttons are large. Add it to the Home Screen if you want an app icon.

## What is inside

1. **詞彙 Vocabulary** — tsunami, drought, earthquake, typhoon, flood, landslides, plus tornado, volcano, and lava. Match pictures, problems, and sentences A–E. Sentence E matches both the flood and the landslides.
2. **動詞 Verbs** — past forms, each used once: brought, damaged, destroyed, killed, knocked down, washed away. Tap or drag them into the disaster scenes.
3. **文法 Since** — reason sentences in the present / present perfect, including Typhoon Ragasa:
   - There are traffic jams since fallen trees have blocked many roads.
   - People are unhappy about the train service since there are not enough trains for all the passengers.
4. **閱讀 Reading** — tornado and volcano encyclopedia facts (over 300 mph / 483 km/h, about 1,200 tornadoes a year in the USA, Tamu Massif, lava about 1,200°C) and short multiple-choice questions.
5. **日記挑戰 Diary** — Jason’s holiday typhoon, then a final quiz.

Stars are saved on this device. Sound is optional and made in the browser (no audio files).

## Answer key

Correct answers follow the corrected Chapter 3 worksheet: the teacher’s marks, and the printed phrase box for `since`. Past-tense rewrites such as “There were traffic jams since fallen trees had blocked many roads” are not accepted. “Because they couldn’t charge them” is not the reason the phones could not be charged.
